# Product notes

## Research status

The product hypothesis is that fragmented discovery, unclear relevance, late timing, and weak tracking cause students to miss useful campus opportunities. The provided interview dataset includes a synthetic/mock batch; that batch must remain labeled synthetic/mock and must not be presented as genuine user research.

## Metrics definitions

- **Relevant discovery rate:** unique opportunities opened / unique opportunities impressed.
- **Save rate:** unique opportunities saved / unique opportunities opened.
- **Action rate:** opportunities marked applied or registered / opportunities saved.
- **Onboarding completion:** completed onboarding / onboarding started.
- **Time to value:** time from onboarding completion to first opportunity opened.

## Experiment hypotheses

1. Showing matching reasons beside the score increases opens and saves versus a score alone.
2. Deadline urgency labels increase action rate for opportunities within 14 days.
3. A short onboarding with explicit interests produces more completed feeds than a longer profile form.
