# Discovery Synthesis

The initial discovery exercise focused on how students currently find and track campus opportunities.

Recurring themes included:
- not knowing where to look
- fragmented information sources
- irrelevant information overload
- finding opportunities too late
- difficulty tracking opportunities after discovery

These themes informed the MVP around:
1. centralized discovery
2. personalized ranking
3. save / tracking
4. deadline visibility

Note: part of the discovery dataset used during product-design practice was synthetic/mock data and is not presented as real user research.
